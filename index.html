    About Me

    I'm a performance engineer. Most of my work comes down to one question: why is this slower than it should be? At Nutanix I was a Member of Technical Staff-4 in Performance Engineering, working on the performance of Nutanix Enterprise AI (NAI).


    That meant building the benchmarking and regression frameworks, then chasing bottlenecks wherever they turned up: GPUs, CPUs, NUMA, networking, storage, distributed inference. The problems I like most are the ones where the bottleneck isn't obvious. Design the experiment, profile it, figure out what's actually happening, fix it, measure again.


    Right now I'm mostly into LLM inference and GPU performance, plus distributed systems, observability and developer tooling. I read papers, contribute to open source when I can and write some of it down on the blog. The goal is to understand these systems all the way from the model down to the hardware.
