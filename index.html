    About Me

    Hi, I'm Arijit. I'm joining Crusoe as a Senior Performance Engineer on the AI Compute team. Most of my work comes down to one question: why is this slower than it should be?

    Before Crusoe, I was a Member of Technical Staff (MTS-4) at Nutanix, working on the performance of LLM inference systems and AI infrastructure. I benchmarked production inference systems and tuned them to scale, with better SLO latency, throughput and GPU utilization. When something was slow, I found out why with micro-benchmarks, profiling and system-level analysis across GPU, CPU, memory, storage and interconnect. And I built the infrastructure around it: regression detection, repeatable performance tests, A/B experiments that showed the impact of every change, and observability, so we could actually see what the system was doing. Before that, I did my Master's in Computer Science at Stony Brook University (SUNY).

    The problems I like most are the ones where the bottleneck isn't obvious. Design the experiment, profile it, figure out what's actually happening, fix it, measure again. I write some of it down on the blog. The goal is to understand these systems all the way from the model down to the hardware.
